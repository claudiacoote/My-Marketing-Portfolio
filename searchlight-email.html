The challenge

Communicating effectively with different customer markets.

Searchlight needed email communications that could support product launches, promotions and essential customer updates while adapting messaging, offers and pricing for different customer groups.

My approach

What I delivered.

I worked with the sales department to produce marketing activity that included product launches, discounts and targeted special offers for customers who had spent above selected thresholds. I independently built campaigns in Mailchimp, designing the emails and writing the copy. The main audience segments were UK and international customers, with pricing adapted for each market. I also supported Lux & Belle, Searchlight’s luxury brand, which was available only to selected customers and required separate positioning and communications.

Mailchimp reporting for one Searchlight audience recorded a 26.0% open rate, 15.9% click rate and 2,119 total clicks. Of those who opened, approximately 61% went on to click, demonstrating strong engagement beyond the initial open. A second Searchlight audience also recorded a 28.8% open rate and 13.4% click rate, providing further evidence of engagement across the email activity I delivered.
