The challenge

Turning the brief into measurable marketing.

The campaign objective was to generate customer leads for Medicus Pharmacy’s NHS repeat prescription service through a short, focused paid-social campaign.

My approach

What I delivered.

The campaign ran for four days with an £85 daily budget (£340 total spend). The supplied Meta results show 141 on-Facebook leads, 20,432 people reached, 568 link clicks and 591 post engagements.
